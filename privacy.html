DJ Eaglizer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DJ Eaglizer is a personal tool used by one person, the owner of the "DJ Eaglizer" YouTube channel (youtube.com/@DJEaglizer), to upload the channel's own original music videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one else uses it.

What the app accesses

The app connects to the owner's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API and Google sign-in. It uses that access only to upload the owner's videos, set their titles, descriptions, schedule and playlists, and check their status.

What data is collected and stored

  • The owner's Google authorization token, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • A local record of the videos the owner has uploaded (video IDs and status).

The app does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers, subscribers or any other users.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to Google and YouTube, as needed to upload and manage the owner's own videos.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
